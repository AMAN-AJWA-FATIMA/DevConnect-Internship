Tomato & Basil Pasta

A quick, comforting pasta with garlic, sweet tomatoes, and fresh basil.

Prep: 10 min Cook: 20 min Difficulty: Easy
Ingredients Method

WHAT YOU NEED

Ingredients

Ingredient quantities shown for 2 servings.

  • 180 g dried pasta
  • 250 g ripe cherry tomatoes
  • 2 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 20 g fresh basil leaves
  • 30 g grated Parmesan (optional)
  • ½ tsp salt, plus more for pasta water
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Tip: keep a mug of pasta water before draining.

LET'S COOK

Method

  1. Cook the pasta

    Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta according to the packet instructions until just al dente. Reserve a mug of pasta water, then drain.

  2. Soften the garlic

    While the pasta cooks, warm the olive oil in a large pan over medium-low heat. Add the sliced garlic and cook for about one minute, without letting it brown.

  3. Make the tomato sauce

    Add the tomatoes and cook for 8–10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they soften and release their juices. Gently press a few tomatoes with the back of a spoon.

  4. Combine and season

    Add the drained pasta, basil, salt, and pepper. Toss well, adding a splash of reserved pasta water until the sauce coats the pasta.

  5. Serve

    Divide between bowls and finish with Parmesan, if using. Serve while hot.
